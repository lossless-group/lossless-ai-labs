## Why Care?

The October comparison was a snapshot, and Instadeck has moved fast since. The
new page sets out what changed and corrects an easy assumption: both generators
write the deck down before designing it, so the order of steps doesn't explain
why their output differs. The handoff from content to design might.

## What Changed

The Collaborations list on the studies page now links to
[Dididecks vs Instadeck: One Month On](https://lossless-group.github.io/lossless-ai-labs/studies/collaborations/dididecks-vs-instadeck-recheck/).
It covers Instadeck's recent progress, its recorded test runs, a side-by-side
of the handoff, the trade-offs on each side, and the limits of the evidence. It
isn't a ranking, and neither system was run for it.

The integration question is parked for now.
