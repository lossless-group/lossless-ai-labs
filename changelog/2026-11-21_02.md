## Why Care?

Several children had moved on since ai-labs last recorded them, so the
splash was showing stale changelogs and docs, and its Context Vigilance Kit
card still described v0.2.0.

***

## What's New?

- **dididecks-ai:** security patches for the splash, deck-export and the
  product frontend, the SvelteKit 3 migration, and a clean type-check.
- **context-vigilance-kit:** v0.4.0, with skill packs, Floorplan as a
  companion, a search handoff to the corpus, and context-v.dev as the kit's
  only address.
- **didi.sh:** two new issues on the invite dashboard and login persistence.
- **event-hog MCP:** contact fix and remove, opening an event in Obsidian,
  and a splash about running on Cloudflare Artifacts.
- **Collaborator study:** the deck-v4 reference clone moves to its
  2026-11-10 handoff.
- **Splash:** the roll-up picks up the new changelogs and docs, and the kit's
  card now says v0.4.0 and that it installs straight from the repo.
