## Why Care?

GitHub flagged the splash's Astro version for two advisories: an XSS through
attribute injection in dynamic tag names (fixed in 7.4.5), and a file read
through the Node image endpoint (fixed in 7.4.7). The splash is a static
Pages build, so neither was reachable in production. The upgrade still clears
the alerts and keeps the build's tooling current.

***

## What's New?

- **Astro ^7.3.8 → ^7.4.13** in `splash/`. `pnpm audit` reports no known
  vulnerabilities, and the build is unchanged at 941 pages.
