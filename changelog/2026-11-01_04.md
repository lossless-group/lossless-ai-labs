## Why Care?

A reader who opened any changelog entry or context-v document saw its title and
lede, then nothing. Search results led to the same empty pages.

## What Changed

Every changelog and context-v page now shows its full text. Callouts, images with
captions, hex-code citations, tables, and code blocks render the way they do on
the context-vigilance-kit splash, and a wikilink to another page on this site is
now a real link.
