## Why Care?

[[2026-11-01_02]] made `studies/context-v/` an Open Knowledge Format bundle.
This pass brings its docs up to the Context Vigilance Kit's frontmatter
template, so the splash and any workflow dashboard can date, credit, and
group them. The five docs from the October collaborations study already
carried the template; the three older docs from May didn't.

## What's New?

- **Last-modified dates from git** on all three, from each file's last commit.
- **A creation date** on the content-authoring-tools study kickoff, from its
  first commit.
- **Authors** on the two docs that had none, from their only human committer.
- **One status onto the kit's list.** The content-authoring-tools kickoff said
  `pre-draft, working discussion`; it's a real, still-open working draft, and
  that study hasn't been pinned yet, so it's `Draft`.

## Under the Hood

- Every edit was made by hand, frontmatter only. No body line changed, no
  existing date moved, and today's date was written into no existing doc.
- The kickoff's legacy `date:` key stays as it is: the splash's content schema
  and date picker still read `date`, so renaming it is left for a later pass.
- The three collaboration plans stay `Ready`. They are parameterized and have
  run twice, once per collaborator, and are ready to run for the next one.

Part of the tree-wide sweep planned in the context-vigilance kit:
`ai-labs/context-vigilance-kit/context-v/plans/Retrofit-Every-Repo-to-OKF-Then-the-Kit-Frontmatter.md`.
