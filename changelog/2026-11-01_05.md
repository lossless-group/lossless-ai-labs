## Why Care?

Skill files follow a format built around `name`, not `title`, and their
reference files often have no frontmatter at all. So about 75 pages showed a
file path where a title belongs, however many frontmatter sweeps ran.

## What Changed

- A doc with no `title` now uses its first `#` heading, then its `name`.
  Lists, search and page headings all pick this up.
- Fifteen scraped pages under a skill's `cache/` folder no longer render. They
  are data, not documents.

The fix is in the splash loader, so the skill files themselves are unchanged.
