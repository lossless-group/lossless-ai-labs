# Operate Like a Team Five Times Your Size — Pitch Narrative (Internal)

> Same pattern as the funder pitches — catchy line, true numbers, citations —
> but the audience is **us**. The decision being pitched: adopt the agent
> operating doctrine now, starting with the grants front, because the campaign
> calendar just made manual coverage impossible.

---

## Beat 1 — The hook

> **"The campaign gave us a surface area problem: $19M to raise, dozens of
> funders to work, and an August deadline. Agents are how a team of three
> covers a big surface."**

$25M goal, $6M committed, founding growth-partner cohort closes **August
2026**. [^57ce95] The remaining $19M lives across dozens of funder
relationships, each wanting research, tailoring, follow-up, and timing. That's
not a headcount problem — it's a coverage problem.

## Beat 2 — The two effects (the thesis slide)

> **"Agents don't just make current work faster. They make impossible-at-our-
> size work possible."**

Effect one: reduce overwhelm on work we already do — research, drafts, prep,
follow-ups. Effect two: enable what we currently triage away — ten outreach
threads at once, conference outreach nine months early, a grant corpus that
drafts new applications on demand. The win is not "same work, faster." It's
"stop triaging away the high-leverage work."

## Beat 3 — The windows we cannot catch manually (OVERWHELM: the deadline wall)

> **"The 2026 funding landscape is a wall of dated windows. Every missed
> deadline is a five-, six-, or seven-figure pass."**

From the mega-gifts landscape scan, live-now windows relevant to Reach:

- **OpenAI People-First AI Fund** — $50M, unrestricted, no AI expertise
  required, **July 2026 deadline**. [^1b000c]
- **Humanity AI** — $500M pool, **$10M open call summer 2026**, labor +
  education pillars. [^08f3c1]
- **BlackRock Future Builders** — $25M national RFP administered by
  JFF. [^a25592]
- **Gates via Digital Promise** — $8M AI tutoring public-goods RFP (opened
  May 2026). [^d2832d]
- **Dollar General Literacy Foundation** — escalating annual adult-literacy
  cycle (~$16M in May 2026). [^90ad13]
- Plus the campaign's own drumbeat: every prospective growth partner needs a
  tailored packet before **August 2026**. [^57ce95]

Tracking, qualifying, drafting, and following up on all of these — while
running the existing pipeline — is precisely the work the team drops today.

*(seek chart: deadline wall — horizontal timeline of open windows with dollar
sizes, "manual capacity" line drawn under it)*

## Beat 4 — Where agents go first (the five fronts, one slide)

> **"Five fronts, one rule: agents research, draft, and watch; humans approve
> and act."**

1. **Grants** — the corpus that drafts itself: reusable snippet library,
   brand voice, deadline calendar, DDQ fill-outs. [^ef2072] [^91f051]
2. **ABM / funder conversations** — research, enrichment, sequencing, reply
   triage so one person tends many threads. [^97bdd0] [^1ffec6]
3. **Events** — the nine-months-ahead game: CFPs, attendee scraping,
   per-meeting prep briefs. [^ccb282] [^7fb84f]
4. **Travel** — itineraries built around stacked funder meetings, route and
   drive-time optimized. [^70a304] [^8a2652]
5. **Social presence** — monitor named stakeholders, draft engagement, human
   posts. LinkedIn explicitly penalizes automated engagement — the guardrail
   is load-bearing. [^9cc232]

The industry data supports the split: ~89.7% of social teams use AI daily, only
~5.4% fully automate. ⚠ VERIFY. [^22d589] Augment, don't abdicate.

## Beat 5 — Proof this already works here (the receipts slide)

> **"The mega-gifts landscape scan was the doctrine in action: 84 verified
> gifts, 81 sources, one afternoon."**

The funding-flows research this pitch cites was produced by exactly this
operating model — parallel research agents, verification against primary
sources, a citation registry, and corpus capture — at a speed and coverage no
manual pass achieves. The doctrine isn't hypothetical; it's how the evidence
under every one of these pitch decks was assembled.

## Beat 6 — The ask (internal decision)

> **"Greenlight one bottleneck: the grants front, corpus-first, before the
> August window."**

Start where the mission is closest and the deadline is real: stand up the
grant corpus + snippet library + deadline calendar, prove it on the live
windows in Beat 3, measure both axes (hours saved *and* asks newly submitted),
then expand front by front. Humans stay on everything outward — sent,
published, submitted. Tool budget is noise against a single caught window.

## Beat 7 — Close

> **"Don't do the same work faster. Do the work we never had time for — while
> the opportunity windows are open, and we still have a team of three."**
