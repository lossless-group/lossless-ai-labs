# Stop Training and Praying — Pitch Narrative

> CEO deck = "what is." [^57ce95] This arc = "what's next, with your gift." The
> door for this audience is the **system**: they've funded workforce programs
> for decades and watched outcomes disappoint. Lead with the diagnosis they
> already suspect, then show the one model the evidence actually endorses.

---

## Beat 1 — The hook

> **"America's workforce system has a name for its own failure mode: train and
> pray."** [^b22ccf]

Workers complete the program, collect the certificate, and hope something
materializes. It's endemic — and the federal government now says so itself. The
2025 *America's Talent Strategy* names the failures explicitly: fragmentation,
credentials without ladders, no accountability for outcomes. [^2e54ee]

## Beat 2 — The cost of praying (simple slide, three numbers)

> **"21 million prime-age Americans are out of the labor force. The system
> built to reach them can't."**

- **21M+** Americans ages 25–54 not participating in the labor force. [^2e54ee]
- **44%** of Americans face at least one major barrier to upskilling —
  childcare, transportation, income disruption — barriers the system was not
  designed to see. [^b22ccf]
- **1.5M** unfilled openings in teaching and healthcare alone — good jobs
  requiring degrees, sitting empty in the workers' own communities. [^57ce95]

And look at *why* the 21 million are out: the vast majority cite disability
or caregiving — for prime-age men, disability is the single largest
factor. [^496b0f] A system built around commuting to employer demand
structurally cannot reach them. Remote work can — it's the documented
accessibility technology behind the record rise in disabled
employment [^272bb5] — which is why a **remote & distributed business
administration** pathway (see [[frontier-job-demand]] and
[[upward-mobility]]) belongs in the workforce system's rebuild: an on-ramp
for exactly the population the current system was never designed to see.

job, with the leak annotated)*

## Beat 3 — The evidence turn (the slide a PhD will screenshot)

> **"We don't have to guess what works. A 3,928-person longitudinal study
> followed young adults for a decade and told us."**

Brookings/Child Trends, tracking people from adolescence to age 29: [^679c9b]

- **Relationship-based work-based learning** (apprenticeship, co-op, mentoring)
  predicts **+0.18** job quality a decade later — the effect *persists* as
  others fade.
- **Non-relationship CTE predicts nothing.** Job shadowing, career majors, tech
  prep: not significant. The supervising-adult relationship is the active
  ingredient.
- **Training at ages 24–27: +0.37.** The single strongest training effect — and
  precisely the Apprenticeship Degree's population.
- **A completed postsecondary degree: +0.90** — the strongest predictor of
  lasting job quality in the entire dataset.

The federal auditor agrees on the delivery model: earn-and-learn means higher
pay, little debt, better retention. [^16a15a]

> **"The Apprenticeship Degree isn't a theory about what might work. It's the
> only model that stacks every feature the evidence endorses — at once."**

*(seek chart: horizontal effect-size bars, one per finding, with the
Apprenticeship Degree's design features mapped onto each)*

## Beat 4 — Proof, not pilot (KPI wall — OVERWHELM #1)

> **"Five years, 3,400 candidates, 550 employers — and the outcomes beat every
> benchmark that matters."** [^57ce95]

93% retention post-first-term · 70% on-time completion vs ~50% nationally ·
65–110% wage gains aide → teacher-of-record · NPS 60+ vs sector benchmark 32 ·
65% Pell-eligible · $75/month maximum out-of-pocket, zero debt. Third-party:
Education Analytics measured a **~2.4 effect size on post-program income** in
Arkansas — causal-analysis-ready at current cohort sizes — with **$20M/yr
projected aggregate wage gains** for current enrollees; Jefferson Parish
credits Reach with halving teacher vacancies. [^57ce95] The field has noticed:
the Urban Institute's scaling framework builds on Reach as lead case, [^7d47c7]
and Inside Higher Ed calls Reach the nation's only accredited
apprenticeship-degree university. [^28ded4]

third-party row added)*

## Beat 5 — Follow the money (OVERWHELM #2: the landscape slide)

> **"Corporate philanthropy just went nine-figure on workforce — and it's
> funding everything around the degree except the degree."**

The 2023 → mid-2026 flows, from our 84-gift landscape scan:

| Funder | Commitment | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| Bloomberg Philanthropies | $250M (2024) | Healthcare high schools — employer-embedded pipelines [^a2ceba] |
| Bloomberg Philanthropies | $90M (2026); $570M cumulative CTE | National Skilled Trades Initiative [^65140c] |
| Lowe's Foundation | $250M by 2035 | 250,000 tradespeople via colleges [^e33e8d] |
| BlackRock Foundation | $100M (2026) | Future Builders; JFF runs a $25M RFP [^a25592] |
| Meta | $115M first year (2026) | America's Workforce Academy, fast-track trades [^e9c685] |
| MacKenzie Scott | $20M → JFF; $42M → 10,000 Degrees | Unrestricted, mobility-screened [^b585ef] [^7ec216] |
| Ascendium | $172M awarded in 2025 | Community colleges, sectoral training, rural learners [^63bbab] |
| Bloomberg / Arnold | $17M HCC · $16M Maricopa (2026) | Largest-ever gifts to community colleges [^a26016] |

Every row funds training, pipelines, or completion **around** the degree. The
piece the system still lacks — a **degree-bearing, employer-embedded,
debt-free** pathway — is exactly what Reach has already accredited and proven.
Walmart's skills-first bet [^2af56e] and Schultz's career-navigation
investments [^f9c42b] reward precisely a model that certifies competence
through work.

*(seek chart: landscape matrix — funder × pipeline-stage, with the
"degree-bearing" column conspicuously sparse except for Reach)*

## Beat 6 — The federal moment (tailwind slide)

> **"Washington just aligned behind apprenticeship. Philanthropy picks who
> scales."**

A 1-million-active-apprentice federal target, Workforce Pell for
outcome-accountable programs, WIOA drift toward pay-for-performance [^2e54ee] —
and community-college apprenticeship sponsors up from **30 (2016) to 200+
(2023)**. [^6f1dc8] Public money follows quality vehicles; philanthropy's
catalytic role is to make sure the highest-evidence vehicle reaches
self-sustaining scale first.

## Beat 7 — What your gift catalyzes (the ask)

> **"$25M turns the proof point into the tipping point — and then the model
> never needs philanthropy again."** [^57ce95]

The campaign math a workforce funder can audit: $25M over 3 years scales
enrollment 3,400 → 7,500+; internal cost $8,037/FTE (below WGU's $8,961 and a
fraction of the $52,900 public 4-year average); braided revenue (Pell +
$75/month + employers) reaches permanent self-funding at 7,500 enrolled;
~$2,000 per candidate per year of philanthropy, then zero. $6M already
committed — Carnegie ($2M) and Schusterman ($1M) anchoring; August 2026 closes
the founding growth-partner cohort. [^57ce95]

You've funded workforce programs that trained and prayed. This is the one the
evidence — and the auditor, and the field's own literature — says will hold.

## Beat 8 — Close

> **"Stop funding training and praying. Fund the model the evidence already
> chose."**
