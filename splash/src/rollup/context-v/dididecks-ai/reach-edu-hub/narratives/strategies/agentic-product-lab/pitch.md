# Charter the Forge — Pitch Narrative

> CEO deck = "what is." [^57ce95] This arc = the infrastructure "what's next":
> not a program for students, but the **shared technology capability the whole
> apprenticeship-degree movement lacks**. The door for this audience is
> infrastructure — funders who'd rather build what every institution uses than
> fund one more pilot.

---

## Beat 1 — The hook

> **"The Apprenticeship Degree runs on a different stack — and nobody built
> it -- not yet...."**

A degree where day-to-day work *is* the curriculum needs software few incumbents ever built: work-and-learning
integration, employer visibility, chunked assessment for people whose learning
windows are shift breaks and commutes — all accreditation-legible. [^4847ba] Legacy
LMS, SIS, and online-program platforms cover a slice at beat. None cover the
whole. The Forge builds what's missing.

## Beat 2 — Who builds software just changed (the shift slide)

> **"Vibe coding moved prototyping from the engineering backlog to the domain
> expert's creativity."**

Until roughly 2025, a domain insight became software only by hiring an
engineering team. Then agentic tools matured to the point where non-engineers
can ship working prototypes — Cursor, v0, Lovable, Bolt, Replit Agent, Claude
Code. [^b71a46] [^8cb194] [^e76b45] Engineers still own hardening, scale, and
security — but they are no longer the gate on *prototyping*. [^3675cf]
⚠ VERIFY the timeline framing before presenting.

The people who actually know what the apprenticeship stack needs —
apprenticeship practitioners, industry-skills experts, adult educators — can
now build the first version of the thing they understand. And the
expert-built version wins on five counts:

- **More accurate product definition.** The product is specified by someone
  who lives the problem — not a requirements doc translated twice before it
  reaches a developer.
- **Closer understanding of the use case.** The constraints that sink generic
  builds — accreditation legibility, shift schedules, a supervisor's real
  day — are known up front, not discovered in month six.
- **Faster to the end-user's hands.** The builder already works beside the
  users; a prototype lands in a real cohort in days, not after a procurement
  cycle.
- **Richer stakeholder feedback.** Apprentices, supervisors, and registrars
  give candid, specific feedback to a trusted colleague — and the revision
  loop is same-week, not next-contract.
- **Authentic motivation.** Not an engineer doing their job — a visionary
  realizing a vision. The builder has waited years for this tool to exist;
  that energy survives the tenth iteration.

## Beat 3 — Why NCAD is the only right home (simple slide)

> **"One network, one lab — so no member rebuilds what another already
> proved."**

NCAD — the National Center for the Apprenticeship Degree, launched by Reach —
already runs partnership development and employer engagement across a member
network, [^97559d] [^a42a6b] [^ef66be] anchoring the 3-million-completions-by-2035
moonshot. [^4aedc1] [^57ce95] Many institutions are hitting the same stack gaps
independently; a neutral center pools the prototypes so the network compounds
instead of duplicating. ⚠ VERIFY current member count.

## Beat 4 — What the Forge builds first (the concrete slide)

> **"Four stack gaps, one distinctive wedge: voice."**

- **Work ↔ credit mapping** — day-to-day work rendered accreditation-legible
  (time-based RA's ~144 hrs/yr related instruction and competency-based
  pathways both [^36c77b]).
- **Employer visibility** — a supervisor dashboard to see and nudge apprentice
  progress.
- **Chunked delivery + assessment** — sized for shifts, not semesters.
- **Evidence capture for accreditation** — messy work artifacts → auditable
  documentation.

The wedge: **voice-first evidence capture.** Teachers and nurses — Reach's
populations — have no time to sit and write; their learning windows are
commutes and between-moments. Speech-to-text turns a spoken post-lesson
reflection into accreditation-ready evidence; text-to-speech turns the commute
into learning time. Only recently good enough, and a genuine differentiator
against text-first incumbents.

## Beat 5 — The funding wave wants shared infrastructure (OVERWHELM: the moment)

> **"AI-in-education money went from millions to billions in 18 months — and
> the pooled funds explicitly want people-centered public goods, not one-off
> pilots."**

The 2025–26 wave from our landscape scan: Microsoft's **$4B Elevate** (20M AI
credentials targeted); [^2d173e] Google's **$1B** for AI training across
accredited nonprofit colleges; [^611b39] the ten-foundation **Humanity AI $500M
pool** — labor and education pillars, first $18M granted May 2026, a **$10M
open call summer 2026**; [^08f3c1] the **$23M AFT National Academy for AI
Instruction** funded by Microsoft, OpenAI, and Anthropic — teachers as the
flagship worker segment; [^8c0ca0] Gates-backed **$8M AI tutoring
public-goods RFP** via Digital Promise; [^d2832d] OpenAI's recurring **$50M
People-First AI Fund** (unrestricted, no AI expertise required, July 2026
deadline); [^1b000c] **NextLadder's $1B** with Anthropic as inaugural AI
partner, chartered for economic-mobility tools. [^5f42a8]

Every one of these favors exactly what the Forge is: a people-centered,
shared-good, education-workforce AI build — proposed by the network that owns
the problem.

*(seek chart: wave timeline 2024→2026 with open-call/deadline flags)*

## Beat 6 — What your gift charters (the ask)

> **"Charter the lab: domain experts prototype, engineers harden, the movement
> keeps the IP."**

It starts with an **Agentic Engineering setup desk and coach** — the Forge's
front door. A member institution's domain expert arrives with a workflow
problem; the desk stands them up on the right agentic tooling (environments,
accounts, guardrails), and the coach walks them from first prompt to working
prototype. Around that front door, the standing capability: domain-expert
builders across the member network, part-time hardening engineering, tool
budget, and governance that keeps outputs shared-good. [^57ce95] Prototype →
test with real cohorts and employers inside member programs → circulate across
the network → harden the winners. Grant-funded, philanthropic framing
throughout — this is shared infrastructure for a movement the funding wave has
already validated, not a commercial bet.

Catalytic frame: a program grant improves one institution. **Chartering the
Forge upgrades every current and future member of the apprenticeship-degree
movement at once** — the 3M-by-2035 moonshot's tooling layer. [^4aedc1]

## Beat 7 — Close

> **"The people who understand the problem can finally build the thing. Give
> them the forge."**
