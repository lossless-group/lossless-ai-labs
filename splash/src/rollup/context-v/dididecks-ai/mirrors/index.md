# Mirrors

Experimental folder. A mirror describes something that is built and working
right now (infrastructure, a deployment, a running pipeline) in a form people
and agents can understand without reading code or opening a dashboard.

A mirror reflects the current state; it doesn't plan or argue. Each one
records `date_mirrored` and the source it was taken from (`mirrors:`). When it
drifts, take it again from that source rather than editing it from memory.

How it differs from its neighbours:

- **A spec** says what we intend to build.
- **A sitemap** maps a surface of our own code.
- **A handoff** captures state at the end of a piece of work.
- **A mirror** reflects what's live, and is re-taken on purpose.
