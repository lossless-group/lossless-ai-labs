## How to use this

Figures below anchor the **my-take** decks' market + comps slides. They are
**directional** — sourced from company disclosures, reputable press, and
market-research aggregators of varying rigor. Citations use LFM hex-code
footnotes (definitions in [Sources](#sources)); cite the linked source, not this
doc, in anything external.

**Thesis these numbers serve:** the barrier to publishing is *editing*. Most
live moments never become content because editing is too slow/costly. EventCut
is AI/agent editing that (1) **unlocks** the vast unpublished/uncaptured volume
and (2) **multiplies** an editor's throughput (10 conference videos → 100).

---

## 1 · Volume of "moments" (the content that isn't getting published)

- **Conferences / trade shows / exhibitions:** ~**32,000 exhibitions/yr
  globally** (318M attendees, ~5M exhibitors),[^tsvf01] with ~**13,000 US trade
  shows/yr**.[^statev1] "Exhibitions" is the clean anchor; the total set of
  business conferences, summits, and corporate events is *multiples* larger.
- **Webinars:** ~**60M+ hosted globally** (2026 est.); a typical company runs
  ~34/yr, and 57–63% of marketers run 50+/yr.[^web60on][^webgc1]
- **Podcasts:** ~**600K active shows** in 2025 (all-time high), out of ~**4.5–4.6M
  total series** ever, with ~**26M episodes published in 2025**.[^pod600][^podst1]
- **Interviews:** *no clean standalone global metric* — interviews live **inside**
  the 26M podcast episodes, webinars, and millions of YouTube talks/panels; treat
  as a slice of those, not its own number.

> The uncaptured tail — everything below the TED/Aspen tier — is the real Jevons
> opportunity: moments that never get captured or published because editing is
> the bottleneck.

---

## 2 · Supply side EventCut unlocks / multiplies (videographers & agencies)

- **Global video-production services market: ~$32.6B (2024) → ~$34.6B (2025)**, on
  **100,000+ active production houses worldwide**.[^vpbri1][^vpggi1]
- **US: ~6,100 video-production establishments** (NAICS 512110),[^ibus512] plus
  **tens of thousands of boutique studios + freelance videographers**; freelance
  video creators **+40% YoY since 2022**.[^vpggi1] This is the population whose
  throughput EventCut multiplies (10 → 100 videos per event).

## 4 · The read for the pitch

- **Pure-play "AI editing removes the friction" comps sit ~$160M–$550M**
  (OpusClip, Veed, Descript, Captions) — EventCut's band today.
- **Generative / avatar tier is $2–5B** (Synthesia, Runway) — a different segment
  (generation, not editing) but the credible up-the-stack ceiling.
- **Horizontal platform ceiling: Canva at $42B.**
- **Tailwind:** AI-video funding **nearly doubled to $3.08B in 2025** (from $1.58B
  in 2024).[^cbfund1]

EventCut's wedge — unlock uncaptured event video + multiply the editor 10→100 —
sits squarely in the fastest-growing AI-editing band, with a legible path up.
