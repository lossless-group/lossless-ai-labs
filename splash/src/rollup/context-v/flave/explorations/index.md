# Explorations

* [The MVP Delta, and What 'A Version Per Audience' Actually Requires](The-MVP-Delta-and-Per-Audience-Versioning.md) - The editor half of the MVP is mostly built. The half that is the product, one document published to several audiences with proof that nothing leaked, has no code yet, two underspecified mechanisms, and one syntax in the spec that does not parse.
* [Positioning Flave Against the Content-Authoring-Tools Study](Positioning-Flave-Against-the-Content-Authoring-Tools-Study.md) - Twenty-three tools, and none of them carries one source through to several audiences with proof that nothing leaked. The study backs flave's core bet, backs most of its spec, and pushed back on exactly one rule, that agents must not write files. That rule is now gone.
* [A Flave Is the Zip for an Agent Session](A-Flave-Is-the-Zip-for-an-Agent-Session.md) - The deliverable you see in a chat is a view. The work is in the sandbox behind it. A .flave can carry all of it, secrets excepted, and it doesn't need Cloudflare Artifacts to exist. Artifacts is the best answer yet to the sharing half.
* [Flave Sync — The Product Is in the Sync Layer](Flave-Sync-The-Product-Is-in-the-Sync-Layer.md) - The format and the instruction kit are free, and every chat that follows them makes .flave files. The business is what keeps track of those files. Dropbox and iCloud can store a .flave. They can't understand it.
