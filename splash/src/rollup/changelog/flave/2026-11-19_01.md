## Why Care?

Flave's primary use case is now an instruction kit: a prompt or link that tells
Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity or a local model to keep its work as a `.flave`
folder and hand it over whenever asked. That makes a lot of `.flave` files,
and the question of where they go. The answer is the product.

## What's New?

- **A new exploration, "Flave Sync: The Product Is in the Sync Layer."** The
  format and kit are free; sync is the business. Find every flave on a
  machine, file chat exports from Downloads as new versions by their `id`,
  offload to save disk space, sync, share, and pull a collaborator's changes.
- **Why not just Dropbox or iCloud.** They store a `.flave` but don't
  understand it:
  - they corrupt version history;
  - they sync your secrets;
  - they can't tell versions apart;
  - merging is all or nothing;
  - sharing exposes raw files instead of the deliverable.
- **The splash says so.** A new section on the home page lays out the five
  reasons and links to the full argument.
