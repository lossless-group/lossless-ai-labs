# The Conversational-UI Study's Profiles Become a Bundle Any Agent Can Read

## Why Care?

This study pins chat clients and native shells (Dive, Kaas, Routa, 5ire, and others) so the cross-product shell can be designed from real source. Its `context-v/` holds the profiles of those repos and the answers to the plan's targeted questions. It is now an Open Knowledge Format (OKF) v0.2 bundle, so any OKF reader can list what's here and what each doc covers without opening a file.

## What's New?

- **All 9 docs declare their `type`**, the Train-Case name of their folder: `Profiles` (8) and `Inquiry` (1).
- **`context-v/index.md`** marks the bundle with `okf_version: "0.2"` and lists its two folders.
- **Two folder indexes**, one line per doc with its lede.

## Under the Hood

- Part of the tree-wide OKF sweep; see the Context Vigilance Kit's plan, [[Retrofit-Every-Repo-to-OKF-Then-the-Kit-Frontmatter]].
- Every edit was made by hand, one file at a time: nine added `type:` lines and nothing removed. No dates moved.
- The pinned upstream repos beside `context-v/` are their own repos and were not touched.
- This is the study's first `changelog/` entry.
