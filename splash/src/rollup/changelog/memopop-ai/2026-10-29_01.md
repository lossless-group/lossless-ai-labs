## Why Care?

The context-vigilance kit now treats every `context-v/` folder as an
[Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format)
(OKF) v0.2 bundle. The rule that makes that true is small: every doc
declares a `type`, the name of the folder it lives in. With it, any OKF
tool can read MemoPop's design record without knowing anything about our
conventions.

This is a compliance change, not a content change. No body line, date,
version, or status was edited.

## What's New?

- **29 docs gain a `type` line** as the last line of their frontmatter:
  27 in `context-v/` (Briefs, Explorations, Handoffs, Issues, Loops,
  Plans, Specs) and 2 in `apps/memopop-native/context-v/`. The 8
  decisions and the connector API spec already declared theirs.
- **Two bundles, two root indexes.** `context-v/index.md` and
  `apps/memopop-native/context-v/index.md` each declare
  `okf_version: "0.2"` and list their folders.
- **One `index.md` per folder**, ten in all, with one line per doc using
  its lede (or the title alone where there is no lede).

`context-v/agent-skills/` stays outside the bundle: skills follow the
Agent Skills spec. `apps/memopop-orchestrator` is its own repo and is
swept separately.

Part of the tree-wide sweep planned in the context-vigilance kit:
`ai-labs/context-vigilance-kit/context-v/plans/Retrofit-Every-Repo-to-OKF-Then-the-Kit-Frontmatter.md`.
