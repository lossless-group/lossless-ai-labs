## Why Care?

The commands made sense one at a time, but the order didn't: which comes first, where loop fits, and what happens after you ship. Now the landing page answers that in one diagram, at [context-v.dev](https://context-v.dev/).

## What's New?

- **"Our recommended flow"** on the landing page.
  - **The build row:** Explore → Decide → Spec → Plan, then Loop (as a team) or Implement (solo), then Reflect.
  - **The learn row:** Reflect leads to Issues, and Issues become Reminders, Blueprints, and Agent Skills: the lasting know-how the next cycle starts with.

  Each step shows its `/cv:` command. It replaces the row of command chips that was there.
- **Drawn with Archify, owned by the site.** The diagram was generated by [Archify](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify) and passed all of its checks. The SVG is inlined into the splash and colored with the site's theme tokens, so it follows light, dark, and vibrant modes. The Archify source sits beside it (`splash/src/diagrams/`) with steps to regenerate.
- **context-v.dev is the canonical address.** Production builds on Vercel now name `https://context-v.dev` in canonical links, the sitemap, `robots.txt`, and `llms.txt`. The README links it.

## Known Gotchas

- **No analytics on context-v.dev yet.** The Vercel project needs `OPENPANEL_CLIENT_ID`, and the OpenPanel client needs `https://context-v.dev` under supported domains.
