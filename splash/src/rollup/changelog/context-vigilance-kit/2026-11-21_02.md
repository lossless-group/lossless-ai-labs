# Skill Packs Get a Folder: Other People's Skills, Linked and Explained

## Why Care?

The best skills are often someone else's: a set on one theme, or one developer's whole setup, like [Garry Tan's gstack](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack) or [Matt Pocock's skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills). Copied into a repo, they pass for your own conventions, stop tracking their source, and leak into anything that reads `context-v/`. We found that out when a copied pack broke the corpus build.

## What changed

- **The rule, by size.** A single third-party skill is a git submodule at `agent-skills/<name>/` and loads like your own. A multi-skill pack is a git submodule at `skill-packs/<name>/`, with an entry in `skill-packs/README.md` saying where it comes from, why it's here and what it does. Never copied files. The `context-vigilance` skill and the folder decision guide both say so.
- **The survey skips packs.** It reads `skill-packs/README.md` and nothing inside a pack, and no longer lists a pack as one of the tree's repos.
- **A pattern page explains why**, with three packs worth knowing, including one drawn from Andrej Karpathy's notes on how coding agents go wrong. The folders page and getting-started link to it.
