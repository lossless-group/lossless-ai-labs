# context-v.dev Is the Kit's Only Address, and Floorplan Joins the Companions

## Why Care?

There are two sites, each for a different reader. context-v.dev introduces the plugin to developers and AI power users. The [corpus](https://lossless-group.github.io/context-v-corpus/) is the full, searchable set of our own context-v docs, for us, collaborators and clients. They link to each other a lot, but they shouldn't share an address or an analytics dashboard.

## What changed

- **The GitHub Pages copy is gone.** The kit's site was also published at `lossless-group.github.io/context-vigilance-kit/`, which put the marketing pages at two canonical URLs and mixed the kit into the corpus's origin. Pages is unpublished, the workflow is deleted (it had `enablement: true` and would have republished on the next push to `master`), and its repo variable is removed. The build now always uses base `/` and `https://context-v.dev`.
- **The kit has its own OpenPanel project**, set on Vercel. The corpus keeps its project, renamed `splash_context-v-corpus`. Before this, the Pages copy had been sending kit traffic to the corpus's project since 2026-10-05. A click from context-v.dev into the corpus now shows in the kit project as an outgoing link, and in the corpus project as a visit referred by context-v.dev.
- **Floorplan is a recommended companion**, next to Graphify, Archify and the Chroma skills. It draws an interactive, plain-language map of a codebase, or a blueprint from a PRD. The README and the getting-started page point at [`haydenschmitty/floorplan`](https://github.com/haydenschmitty/floorplan) and its own plugin install commands.
