## Why Care?

The market standard is three parts, `major.minor.patch`, and the skill called
four parts the one right answer. Anyone installing the kit shouldn't have to
adopt our format, but agents do need to read both correctly: the `1` in `0.1.0`
is a minor, and the `1` in `0.1.0.0` is a major.

## What's New?

- **Both formats are valid.** The user picks and nothing enforces it.
- **Agents count the parts first,** keep the format a doc or repo already uses,
  and never convert between them. Published packages always use three.
- **The source of epochs is credited:** Anthony Fu's
  [Epoch Semantic Versioning](https://antfu.me/posts/epoch-semver). His format
  stays at three numbers, so `1002.3.0` means epoch 1, major 2, and agents can
  read that too.

Templates still start at `0.0.0.1`, and no existing version changed.
