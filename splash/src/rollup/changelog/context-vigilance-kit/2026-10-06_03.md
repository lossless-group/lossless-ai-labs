## Why Care?

The landing page opened with the argument for treating context like code, but never said the simplest thing: the whole practice is one folder. Now it does, before anything else.

## What's New?

- **A new opening band** above The Hook: *A dead simple, highly flexible, surprisingly effective pattern for context & memory. Just add a folder to every repo: `context-v/`*, beside a small repo tree with `context-v/` highlighted.
- **An "OKF Compliant" stamp** in that band, linking to the [OKF profile pattern](https://context-v.dev/patterns/okf-profile/).
- **A smaller hook headline**, so the band and the hook don't compete.
- **The invitation is rewritten** as "A common pattern, openly shared": we've used context-v across 40+ repos, it has worked better for us than Spec Kit and OpenSpec, steal what you like, abandon what you don't. MPL-2.0 license.
