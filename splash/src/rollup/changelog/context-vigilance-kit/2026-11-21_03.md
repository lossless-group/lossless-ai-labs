## Why Care?

Specs say what you mean to build, and blueprints say how it's designed.
Nothing said what's actually running: which services are up, what they're
built from, what they cost. People and agents had to read code or click
through a dashboard to find out. `sitemap/` came close, but only for
websites.

***

## What's New?

- **`mirrors/` is an experimental folder.** A mirror reflects what's built
  and live: a deployment, infrastructure, a service topology. It records
  `date_mirrored` and its source (`mirrors:`), and ends with how to
  re-take it. When it drifts, re-take it from the source instead of editing
  it from memory.
- **The skill, the folder guide and the Folders and Modes page** describe
  it. The guide adds two confusions: blueprint vs. mirror, and sitemap vs.
  mirror.
- **`sitemap/` is the website version of a mirror.** Existing sitemaps
  stay as they are; a later migration may fold them in.
- **`/cv:init`** doesn't create `mirrors/` up front, like the other
  experimental folders, and **the survey map** lists it.
