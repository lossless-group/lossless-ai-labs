# Context-V Speaks the Open Knowledge Format

## Why Care?

Context-v has always been a folder of markdown files with YAML frontmatter. So is the [Open Knowledge Format](https://github.com/GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format) (OKF), an open spec from Google Cloud for knowledge that people write and agents read. They turned out to fit together almost exactly. Now a `context-v/` folder is meant to be readable by any OKF tool, with no translation step.

## What's New?

- **A decision, logged where decisions go.** `context-v/decisions/` exists now, and its first doc says context-v is a *profile* of OKF v0.2: OKF's few requirements, with context-v's folders, lifecycle, versions, and IDs on top. It names the alternatives we passed over and what's still open.
- **Every doc declares a `type`, and it's the folder's name:** `type: Specs`, `type: Explorations`, `type: Decisions`, `type: Loops`. It's never a judgment call, so a check can verify it.
- **Folders are plural, and new ones are welcome.** The canonical set is a starting point. Create `narratives/` or `research-notes/` when the work calls for it, and its docs carry that name as their type. `extra/` and `sitemap/` stay as the long-standing singular exceptions.
- **Our lifecycle maps onto OKF's.** OKF's `status` is draft, stable, or deprecated; context-v keeps its richer one and publishes the mapping.
- **README** has a new section on folders, types, and OKF, and links the decision.
- **On the site:** a new [[okf-profile|Context-v is an OKF bundle]] pattern, and the folders and frontmatter patterns now cover `type` and open folders.

## Known Gotchas

- **Not built yet.** The templates, `/cv:new`, and the frontmatter check don't set or require `type` yet, and the root `index.md` that replaces `context-v/README.md` isn't written. The decision doc lists these as the next step.

## What's Next?

- Build the decision: `type` in templates and the check, and the OKF-shaped `index.md`.
- Open the kit's own `context-v/` in OKF's reference visualizer as a conformance test.
