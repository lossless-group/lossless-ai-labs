## Why Care?

We design decks as scroll pages because that's where layout and illustration come out best on the web. Play decks and PDFs exist for presenting and for sending. When a client wanted the deck on their own site, though, only the play deck was easy to embed. The scroll deck carried our dev tools, and its post-NDA pages had to be cut by hand.

## What's New?

`deck-export html` already packed a scroll page into one self-contained file with its CSS, fonts and images inlined. Three new flags make that file fit to ship:

- **`--public`** drops every section marked `data-tier="post-nda"`, so the export holds only what can go out before an NDA.
- **`--clean`** removes the slide-rating pill, dev overlays and inline scripts, then adds one small reader runtime: arrow keys, double-click and scroll to move, an `N / total` counter, `#N` links, and a light/dark toggle.
- **`--title`** sets the document title.

The first use is Humain Ventures' firm deck, which humain-web now serves behind its LP passcode, as a scroll deck by default with play and PDF alongside.
