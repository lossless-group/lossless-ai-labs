## Why Care?

The shell loads each client site's deck registry with esbuild, and it
assumed esbuild always came along with Vite. Vite 8 bundles with Rolldown
and doesn't bring it, so reach-edu-hub's build broke on the upgrade. Every
other client site would have hit the same failure on its own Vite 8 move.

***

## What's New?

- **@lossless-group/dididecks-shell 0.4.1** is published to GitHub's
  registry, with `esbuild` as a real dependency.
- **It also ships the player split** from 2026-10-28: the shell's viewer
  components re-export from `@lossless-group/dididecks-player`, now a
  dependency.
- **dididecks-player 0.1.0 is on GitHub's registry too**, the same tarball
  as on npmjs. Client sites route every `@lossless-group/*` package to
  GitHub, so without it 0.4.1 couldn't install.
- **reach-edu-hub is the first site on 0.4.1.** Its build and its Vercel
  deploys pass.

From now on, a player release has to go to both registries while client
sites resolve `@lossless-group/*` from GitHub.
