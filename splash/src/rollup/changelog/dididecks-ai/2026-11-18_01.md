## Why Care?

The splash was the first one we built for DidiDecks, back in May, and it hadn't
kept up. It read like a spec, it had no search, and it couldn't be shared well:
no share image at all. Worse, nobody noticed it had stopped deploying. Every
push since May 12 had failed, so the changelog and context-v pages added on
November 1 never went live.

It now reads like the newer Lossless splashes (ai-labs, astro-knots) while
keeping DidiDecks' own ink-and-brass look.

## What's New?

- **A home page that says what DidiDecks is for.** "Decks that pass due
  diligence," then the bar a deck has to clear, the six-step loop from ingest
  to refresh (with the `/remake-deck` diagram), what's built, the two product
  bets and seven rules, and where to read more.
- **Toolkit cards** for the deck shell, the player, `/remake-deck`, deck
  export, slide archetypes and citations, most linking to the ship note that
  introduced it. Adding a card is adding a file.
- **Search** across every changelog entry and context-v doc, and sort controls
  on both lists.
- **Light, dark and vibrant modes**, with dark as the default.
- **A share card for every page.** A tall 3:4 image for chat apps and a wide
  one for X, drawn at build time. The home card is the bankers at the safe.
- **Entries render through LFM**, so callouts, tables and citations show
  properly.
- **llms.txt and llms-full.txt** for agents and LLMs.
- **Analytics** through OpenPanel, in production only.

## Under the Hood

- The structure, header, search, sort and markdown renderer come from
  `ai-labs/splash`. The palette and type (brass, ember, Cormorant Garamond over
  Inter) stay DidiDecks' own, now as two-tier tokens in `theme.css`.
- Share cards follow `astro-knots/sites/cathode-site`: satori lays out each
  card, resvg rasterizes it, sharp writes the JPEG. One og:image per page, the
  tall one.
- Changelog and context-v still come straight from this checkout and from
  public children over the GitHub API. Client deck repos are never read, and
  anything marked `publish: false` stays off the site, so none of the 46
  context-v docs held back in August appear here.
- The deploy failed because the workflow pinned pnpm 9 while `package.json`
  asks for pnpm 10.15. The pin is gone.
- Dates were showing a day early west of Greenwich; they're now formatted in
  UTC.
