# Eight pitch decks, one citation discipline

## What shipped

**The pitch-generation strategy decks.** Eight Scroll-UI decks at corpus-aligned
slugs — `upward-mobility` (new flagship), `workforce-development`,
`rural-income-boosts`, `adult-literacy-numeracy`, `frontier-job-demand`,
`ncad-forge`, `agent-workflow-maxxing`, `reverse-engineer-funding` — each built
from a funder-facing pitch narrative (`context-v/narratives/strategies/*/pitch.md`)
that balances tweet-worthy core lines with KPI walls and "overwhelm" evidence
slides. The CEO's June 2026 campaign deck is the "what is" anchor; these are
"what's next, with your gift."

**Citations that survive reordering.** A per-client registry
(`context-v/narratives/citations/registry.csv`, ~190 sources) assigns every
unique article a permanent hex code. Each deck carries a `citations.ts` that
maps codes to sequential `[n]` markers by first appearance — reorder slides and
the pairing holds. Inline markers get hover popovers; every deck ends with a
paginated Sources slide (`SourcesPager`, new) that stays inside the 16:9
viewport with ▲/▼ paging.

**Verified numbers.** Research agents fetched primary sources for every flagged
figure. Casualties included a fabricated "PwC drone survey" stat, decade-stale
Mellon/RWJF grant totals, a mislabeled global-as-US drone market size, and a
"94% skills gap" that was actually a partnership stat. Giving USA, Cerulli, and
Altrata were updated to current editions. The decks now cite what the sources
actually say.

**Presentation mode.** On scroll decks the site header hides until the cursor
reaches the top of the screen (`body.is-scroll-deck` + `deck-header-autohide.js`);
slides get the full viewport; the shell paginator owns the top-right corner
(the redundant per-slide kicker is hidden); `c` now clears *all* review chrome
including the rank pill. Typography was resized for funder-age eyes at
presentation distance: wider headline measures, larger card copy and
subheaders, and legible chips — including retiring hairline Courier New for a
modern system-mono stack at the theme-token level.

**Renames with receipts.** Deck slugs now match the reach-edu corpus strategy
slugs; old strategy section components are archived under
`src/layouts/sections/_archive/`; old scroll routes are removed (registry
entries preserved as `ARCHIVED_STRATEGY_DECKS`). The literacy deck was retitled
along the way — "guarantee" is not a word for education; it is now
**"Foundational Capabilities, Measured Improvements."**
