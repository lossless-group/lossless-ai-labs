## Why Care?

GitHub's dependency graph had been off for this repo, so its security
alerts were months out of date: fixed problems stayed open and parts of the
repo were never checked. With the graph back on, the list now matches the
code.

***

## What's New?

- **46 stale alerts closed themselves** once GitHub re-read the splash
  lockfile.
- **deck-export:** `image-size` forced to 2.x. pptxgenjs declares 1.x but
  never loads it, and 1.x has no fix. PPTX export still works.
- **Frontend:** `@sveltejs/adapter-vercel` 5 → 6.3.5 and SvelteKit to
  2.70.4. The build passes.
- **Still open:** two low-severity SvelteKit alerts that are fixed only in
  SvelteKit 3, a major upgrade we'll do separately.
