## Why Care?

The plan existed as one person's finished document, its build scripts in a
gitignored `extra/` folder, and a page on a personal site. None of it was
reusable by anyone else in the cohort, or by an agent coaching through the 100 days.

## What's New?

- **A blank PSP template**, reconstructed from the finished plan because no
  blank copy of the program's form was kept.
- **A scored goal block**: what counts, how it's scored, rules, and a
  Floor / Target column, so each week is checkable on inputs rather than outcomes.
- **`build-psp.sh` takes any plan** instead of one hardcoded file.
- **A template anatomy** separating the program's sections from member extensions.
- **A skill stub**, `personal-strategic-plan`, with the workflows still to write.

## What's Next

Write the skill's workflows: drafting from the blank form, daily check-ins,
the weekly retro that rewrites the remaining weeks, and 30/60/90 readings.
