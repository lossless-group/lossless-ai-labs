## Why Care?

The cohort will mostly share this link in chat. WhatsApp and iMessage favor
tall cards, and the splash only had a landscape banner.

## What's New?

- **Six formats plus the Default alias**: Banner (1200×630), BannerTall (3:4),
  BannerTallMax (2:3), Portrait (4:5), PortraitTall (9:16), Square, all as
  `public/ogimage__Hope-Ai--<Format>.jpg`.
- **Rendered from the hero, not illustrated.** One template,
  `splash/scripts/og-template.html`, lays itself out for any size: copy left
  and sun right in landscape, a two-line headline over a large sun in square
  and tall. `splash/scripts/render-og.sh` renders the set with headless
  Chrome, archiving any file it replaces in `.ogimage-archive/`.
- **Cache-busted `og:image`** (`?v=2`), since the banner's bytes changed at a
  stable URL.
