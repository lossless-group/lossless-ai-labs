## Why Care?

A plan nobody tracks fades by week three. Members also need to show their
coach and cohort where they stand without walking them through the plan.

## What's New?

- **`references/dashboard-guidelines.md`**: data and views, not a
  technology.
  - Two files: `tracker.yaml` (the plan as numbers: habits with per-week
    floors and targets, outcomes with Day 30/60/90 milestones) and one
    `log/week-NN.yaml` per program week.
  - Three levels: days roll up into 7-day program weeks, weeks into the
    checkpoints.
  - Two views: the detailed week for the member, the goals × weeks summary
    with checkpoint cards for coach and cohort.
  - Exact status rules, including the two that matter most: unlogged is
    never a miss, and a week in progress never reads as a failure.
- **Agent-agnostic by design.** Any assistant, or a person with a text
  editor, can log. Daily, weekly, or catching up days later all work;
  entries go under the date they happened.
- **Templates**: `templates/tracker.yaml` and `templates/log-week.yaml`.
- **A reference implementation** in `references/rendering/dashboard/`,
  copied from mpstaton.com/psp/dashboard: renderer-agnostic rollup logic in
  `tracker.ts`, the Astro views, and a fictional sample dataset.
- **The skill** gains *Logging* and *Build a dashboard*, and its check-ins
  no longer assume a daily rhythm or a particular assistant.
