## Why Care?

Most of the cohort uses the Claude desktop app on Mac or Windows, not a
terminal. A repo full of markdown does nothing for them unless Claude can
read it. The splash gives them the one thing they need: a message to paste.

## What's New?

- **A splash at `lossless-group.github.io/hope-ai/`.** The hero holds a
  ready-to-paste message for Claude with the repo and skill links, and a
  copy button.
- **A downloadable skill.** `scripts/package-skill.sh` zips the skill with
  its templates and references in the layout the Claude apps expect, and the
  splash build runs it so the download is always current. The page walks
  through installing it (Settings → Capabilities, then Customize → Skills →
  Upload a skill), per Anthropic's help center.
- **The skill now works.** It was a stub; it now covers drafting a plan one
  goal at a time, tightening vague goals, morning and evening check-ins, the
  weekly review, and exporting back to the cohort's Google Doc.
- **A brand.** A half-risen sun over a horizon as the mark, "hope" in italic
  serif with ".ai" in mono as the logotype, a 128×128 app icon, favicon, and
  a 1200×630 share image.
- **A "first light" look**, distinct from its siblings: light-default dawn
  palette, a hero sun ringed by 100 day-ticks that light up on load, index
  cards borrowed from the plan's notecard system.

## Also Fixed

The mode toggle inherited from the id-didi-sh splash saved the chosen mode
under a different key than the layout read, so the choice never survived a
reload. Both now use `hope-ai-splash-mode`.
