## Why Care?

The first entry shipped the toolkit under the wrong name, private, and missing
the scaffolding its siblings in `ai-labs` all share. This one makes it a
normal member of the tree.

## What's New?

- **Renamed to `hope-ai`**: the GitHub repo, the folder, and the submodule
  entry in the parent `ai-labs`. The skill link in `~/.claude/skills/`
  follows the new path.
- **Public**, like every other `lossless-group` repo in the tree.
- **`development`, `main`, and `master`**, per the three-tier branch model.
- **`AGENTS.md`**, with `CLAUDE.md` symlinked to it: what the repo is, its
  layout, and the rule that no member's filled-in plan or identity details
  ever land here now that it's public.
- **`context-v/`** gains `specs/`, `plans/`, `issues/`, and `explorations/`
  beside `agent-skills/`.
- **Registered** in the `ai-labs` children table.

## What's Next

A splash page, then the skill's coaching workflows.
