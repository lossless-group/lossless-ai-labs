# A Spec for a Shared PSP Workspace Your Coach and Group Can See

## Why Care?

A 100-day plan held alone is easy to avoid. The program already pairs every member with a coach, puts them in a small power group, and runs the cohort as a team, but the toolkit so far is single player: your plan lives in your chat or your downloads, and nobody sees your progress unless you paste it somewhere. Logging a day should be as easy as saying it to your assistant, and the right people should be able to see it and respond.

## What's New?

- **A stub spec**, [[Shared-PSP-Workspace-with-Circles-and-a-Native-Connector]]. It isn't scheduled for build yet. It sets out a first proposal to argue with:
  - **Circles.** Self, dyad (member and coach), power group and team. Circles are stored as data, so other group shapes fit without code changes.
  - **Storage.** One versioned repo per member that only they write to, one repo per circle for discussion, and a small database for who belongs where and who can see what.
  - **Sharing on the member's terms.** Each goal can be private, or visible to the dyad, the power group or the team. The server checks permissions on every read instead of leaving it to the assistant, and nothing is posted without the member's approval.
  - **Discussion.** Posts, replies, reactions and feeds within each circle.
  - **One connector for the native apps.** It works from Claude or ChatGPT on desktop or phone, and signing in is by invite.
  - **Starting again without penalty.** A new Day 0 keeps the history, and days that weren't logged never count as failures.
- The spec ends with **four build phases and a list of open questions**: program norms on sharing, hosting, names, and how the workspace relates to the program's shared Google Doc.

## Under the Hood

- The storage and connector pattern comes from event-hog ([lossless-group/event-hog-mcp](https://github.com/lossless-group/event-hog-mcp)): a Cloudflare Worker serving a remote MCP server, Cloudflare Artifacts repos where every write is an attributed commit, and D1 for structured data. The spec adds what event-hog doesn't have: identity, nested groups and permissions.
- mpstaton-site's PSP dashboard ([lossless-group/mpstaton-site](https://github.com/lossless-group/mpstaton-site)) supplies the data shape and the dashboard components that would read from the new store.
- `context-v/specs/index.md` lists the new spec.
