  The installable practice. The `cv` Claude Code plugin gives your agent the context-vigilance skill, `/cv:*` workflow commands that each put it in a role, templates, and a starter scaffold. No database, no config, no server. It installs straight from the repo with `/plugin marketplace add`. v0.4.0 adds a home for other people's skill packs, Floorplan as a recommended companion, and a site search that carries your query over to the corpus. The tree-wide corpus it grew out of now lives separately in context-v-corpus, with its public slice searchable online.
